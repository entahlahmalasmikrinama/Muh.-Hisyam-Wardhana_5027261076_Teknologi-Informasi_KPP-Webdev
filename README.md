# TechJobs
TechJobs Portal is a digital platform designed to bridge the gap between technology talents and companies or startups seeking qualified employees.

Through this platform, job seekers can discover internship and employment opportunities based on their skills, interests, and career aspirations. Meanwhile, companies can identify potential candidates by reviewing their profiles and CVs, making the recruitment process more efficient.

The idea was inspired by the Indonesian government's target of creating 19 million jobs. However, while job creation is important, finding employment that genuinely aligns with an individual's skills and career goals remains a challenge.

TechJobs aims to address this issue by connecting the right talents with the right opportunities.
